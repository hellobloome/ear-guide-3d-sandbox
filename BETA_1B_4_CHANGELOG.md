## New
- Locked Adrenal using the approved Ear 1 calibration coordinate.
- Added two calibration modes:
  - point placement
  - Occiput area calibration
- Area mode supports up to 10 numbered boundary taps.
- Added Undo Last, Clear Area and Copy Occiput Area Data.
- Point and area calibration modes are mutually exclusive.
- `data/3d-points-beta.json` now supports `point` and `area` location types.
- Occiput is registered as a pending area rather than a single point.

## Suggested Occiput workflow
Use about 4–6 boundary taps around the region. More taps are available if the shape needs extra detail.
